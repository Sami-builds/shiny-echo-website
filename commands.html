🎮Fun Commands

  • !joke

    Tells a random joke

  • !funfact

    Shares a fun fact

  • !fortune

    Gives you a fortune cookie message

  • !wyr

    Posts a “Would You Rather” question to get chat talking. Shiny Echo does not currently track or reply to answers.

  • !roll

    Roll a die (1–6)

  • !pick option1|option2|option3

    Picks between options. Example: !pick pizza|burger|tacos

  • !8ball

    Gives a Magic 8 Ball answer

  • !rps

    Plays rock, paper, scissors
